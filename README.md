a custom PCB, firmware, a test bench, and a fair amount of debugging. I work
across hardware design, embedded software, FPGA/Linux systems, TinyML, and
computer vision.

## Currently

  system myself, including the custom hardware, firmware, and on-device
  inference.
- Serving as President of **[GeekPie Association](https://github.com/ShanghaitechGeekPie/)**

- Working with **Prof. Fei Fang at Carnegie Mellon University and WWF** on a

## A few projects

- **[MIKU LA32R Out-of-Order SoC](https://github.com/TossACoinTAC/NSCSCC2026-MIKU)**
  is a four-issue SpinalHDL processor and FPGA/Linux system. I captained the
  GeekPie_ team and worked on the CPU, FPGA integration, and Linux bring-up.
  [CPU development](https://github.com/Maotechh/nscscc-cpu) ·
  [Linux kernel](https://github.com/Maotechh/nscscc-linux-kernel)
- **[TI Cup Ball-Balancing Vehicle](https://maotechh.github.io/projects/ti-cup.html)**
  combines K230 vision, STM32F407 control, and a custom mechanical system. I
  worked on the vision and mechanical integration as team captain.
- **[SparkLive](https://github.com/SkillfulPainter/Sparklive-Studio)** is a
  local multimodal livestream project for NVIDIA DGX Spark. I contributed to
  system integration and local deployment.
  [Digital-human workspace](https://github.com/SkillfulPainter/Sparklive-Digital-Man)
