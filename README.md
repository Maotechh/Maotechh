### Hey, I'm Hanze (`Maotechh`) 👋

CS undergrad at ShanghaiTech and Chair of **[GeekPie Association](https://geekpie.club/)**.  
I'm moving toward **computer architecture** and **AI inference hardware**, with hands-on work across RTL, FPGA, Linux, custom PCBs, firmware, and system validation.

## Selected highlights

- **[NSCSCC 2026 Loongson Cup](https://github.com/TossACoinTAC/NSCSCC2026-MIKU) · National Second Prize**<br>
  Captained a four-person team building **MIKU**, a four-issue out-of-order LA32R SoC in SpinalHDL. I worked on memory access, branch prediction, timing optimization, FPGA integration, and Linux 5.14 / Buildroot bring-up. The complete system reached 100 MHz on Artix-7. [CPU](https://github.com/Maotechh/nscscc-cpu) · [Linux kernel](https://github.com/Maotechh/nscscc-linux-kernel)

- **[TI Cup 2026 Shanghai Undergraduate Electronics Design Contest](https://maotechh.github.io/projects/ti-cup.html) · First Prize**<br>
  Led the vision and mechanical integration of a ball-balancing vehicle. I implemented K230 ball tracking and geometric calibration, designed the K230–STM32F407 interface, and integrated the camera and actuators. The vision pipeline reached 88.9 FPS with about 1 mm static calibration error.

- **[IEEE ICPS 2026](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICPS70486.2026.11567971) · Best Paper Award**<br>
  Co-first author of *Energy Management Design Guide for Self-powered Ambient IoT Devices*. We developed a source–storage–load design model and reduced power consumption by 60% on a photovoltaic-powered prototype. [Award announcement](https://sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/2026/0527/c2858a1123090/page.htm)

- **[NVIDIA DGX Spark Hackathon](https://github.com/SkillfulPainter/Sparklive-Studio) · Excellence Award**<br>
  Captained the SparkLive team and contributed to system integration and local deployment of a multimodal livestream assistant using Qwen, Whisper, FastAPI / WebSocket, and OBS control.
